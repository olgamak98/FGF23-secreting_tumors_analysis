In this work we analyzed phosphaturic mesenchymal tumors (PMTs), which are rare neoplasms causing tumor-induced 
osteomalacia through excessive production of fibroblast growth factor 23 (FGF23), but the molecular mechanisms 
underlying FGF23 overexpression remain incompletely understood.

We performed integrated genomic and transcriptomic profiling of PMTs using whole-exome and bulk RNA sequencing 
together with reanalysis of independent bulk and single-cell RNA sequencing datasets.

Our findings expand the genomic spectrum of PMTs and suggest that distinct molecular alterations converge on 
common signaling mechanisms driving pathological FGF23 production.
